Event Rules

General Tile Rules

  • Any tile may be completed by multiple players. Tiles are not assigned to a single person.
  • Some tiles may share similar rewards or item categories, such as Ancestral, Virtus, Masori, or SRA pieces.
  • Duplicate item submissions for separate tiles are not allowed.
  • If one tile is completed using a specific piece, another tile requiring that same set must use a different valid piece.
  • Example: if Ancestral Hat is submitted for one tile, another Ancestral tile must use a different valid piece, such as Body or Legs.
  • Skilling pet and bossing pets may be used as a free-choice completion for any one tile. One time use (one boss, one skill). Must be used immediately.
  • Any megarare obtained (Scythe of Vitur, Twisted Bow, Tumeken's Shadow) will award a ONE-TIME Revive. This may be used at any time to revive a shot tile, requiring the opposing team to complete it again. This can be utilized ONCE per unique megarare (Twisted Bow, Scythe, Shadow). You cannot revive a tile again once it has already been revived once, and you cannot revive a tile if its entire boat has already been sunk.
  • Skotizo and Chompy pets do not count.
  • Skotizo drops do not count toward any tile completion.
  • Fishing Barrel, Tackle Box, Acorns, and Forestry-related items require a before screenshot.
  • Raid drops require the screenshot to be taken directly at the reward chest.
  • Inferno and Quiver collection log screenshots must visibly include the collection log interface.
  • RuneLite automatic screenshots are highly recommended.

Battleship Rules

  • Each team captain places all 6 ships on their team’s hidden board before the event begins.
  • Teams will not know the enemy board layout until captains have placed their ships and gameplay begins.
  • Ships may be rotated and positioned however the captain chooses, provided placements remain valid.
  • Completing a task marks the corresponding tile as completed on the board.
  • Whether that completed tile is a HIT or MISS depends on whether the opposing team placed a ship on that tile.
  • HIT means the opposing team had a ship on that tile.
  • MISS means the opposing team did not have a ship on that tile.
  • The live board and shot logs update automatically for all viewers in real time.
